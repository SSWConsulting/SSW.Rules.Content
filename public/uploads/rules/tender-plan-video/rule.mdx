The Bid Owner approves a tender at Gate 1, then often sees nothing until the draft response arrives a few days before close. By then it is too late to change the solution, the features, or the price without a scramble.

<endIntro />

Soon after Gate 1, the Pre-sales Solution Architect records a short Tender Plan video. It is often the first real insight that the Bid Owner and the board get into the bid.

## When to record it

Record it on the day after Gate 1 (day 3 in [the tender timeline](/tender-timeline)), before the team starts writing. Feedback at this point is cheap. Feedback in the last week costs the team a late night.

## What to show

Keep it to about 5 minutes:

1. **The client's problem** - in 1 or 2 sentences
2. **The features you need to make** - what the client asked for, and which of those features you will show in the POC
3. **The proposed solution** - the products and architecture, at a high level
4. **The commercials** - pricing model, hosting, and the team you plan to use
5. **Risks and open questions** - what could stop you winning or delivering

Show, do not tell. A quick mock-up of the planned proof of concept (POC) is worth more than slides.

## Where to put it

* Save it in the tender's document folder, next to the [one-page summary](/tender-one-page-summary)
* Email the link to the Bid Owner and the Bid Team
* Put the link in the bid plan, so anyone who joins later can catch up
