A support chat dashboard says accuracy fell to 71% and recommends a returns prompt update. It also calls the assistant a success, with a record 94% of customers helped. Neither figure says how many answers anyone checked, or out of what. Three topics have never been checked at all, and nothing on the page says so.

That's why you should **show how much was checked, out of what, beside every AI quality rate**.

## Show the funnel before the rate

Northwind Traders counts customer questions for 8–14 Oct. Show each step with its definition under the number:

* **Eligible: 1,240** - Questions in chats where the assistant is switched on
* **Attempted: 1,105** - The assistant tried to answer; 135 went straight to a person
* **Answered: 870** - The assistant gave an answer; another 235 went to a person mid-chat
* **Checked in the random sample: 61** - Random answers from five topics, no two from the same chat. A support agent answered each question without seeing the AI answer

The funnel shows 370 handovers that a single agreement tile hides.

## Put coverage beside every rate

Next to 47/61 agreement (77%), show "Checked 61 of 870 answers (7%)" and label it as the random sample. Those 61 checks come from 718 answers in five topics, not every topic the assistant handles. The 18 targeted checks get their own count.

## Keep the denominator honest

* **Set it before you read the trend** - The bad dashboard's "71%, −15% vs last week" adds 18 targeted returns checks to this week only (56/79), while last week's 55/64 had none, so the two weeks no longer measure the same thing. To test whether a change is real, see [Do your dashboards show whether a change is real?](/dashboard-changes-real-or-noise)
* **Don't define it by the outcome** - "Customers helped 94%" is 818/870. It counts only questions the assistant answered, so all 370 handovers drop out
* **Count independent cases** - Northwind never checks two answers from the same chat, so each check is a separate case. Answers in one chat share a customer and a topic, so 61 answers from a handful of chats can carry less evidence than 61 separate checks. A range that ignores that may be too narrow
* **Keep targeted checks apart** - Returns chats picked after complaints show 9/18 agreed. Show that beside the rate, not inside it. Targeted checks show where to look, not how often the assistant is right

When checked answers disagree, see [Do you turn AI quality disagreements into a review inbox?](/disagreement-inbox-for-ai-quality).

## List what has never been checked

Rank topics with no checks by volume, so review effort goes where the answers are:

* **Gift cards and vouchers** - 71 answers
* **Bulk and wholesale orders** - 48 answers
* **Allergens and ingredients** - 33 answers

That's 152 answers, 17% of everything answered. These topics went live on 1 Oct and aren't in the review sample yet, so the 77% says nothing about them. Add them to the sample before quoting one figure for the whole assistant.
