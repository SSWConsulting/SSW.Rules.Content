An AI quality dashboard says accuracy fell to 71% and customers helped reached 94%. It gives neither denominator. Three topics have never been checked, while complaint-led checks are mixed into this week's headline. The page cannot support a whole-assistant verdict.

**Show how much was checked, out of what, beside every AI quality rate.** Name the sampled population and expose what the checks leave out.

<endIntro />

These Northwind Traders dashboards use example data.

## Show the funnel before the rate

For 8–14 October, Northwind counts:

* **Eligible: 1,240** — Questions in chats with the assistant switched on
* **Attempted: 1,105** — AI tries to answer; 135 go straight to a person
* **Answered: 870** — AI supplies an answer; another 235 go to a person mid-chat
* **Random checks: 61** — Randomly drawn from 718 answers in five included topics, with no two checks from the same chat

The funnel reveals 370 handovers. **Customers helped 94%** means 818/870 answered questions in the bad example; it excludes all handovers and does not establish usefulness. Define the success criterion and population before naming a rate.

For the checks, a support agent answers without seeing AI's answer. Matching answers measure agreement, not proven correctness; [adjudicate disagreements against evidence](/disagreement-inbox-for-ai-quality).

## Put both coverage figures beside agreement

Show **47/61 agreement (77%)**, **61/718 checked in the sampling frame (8.5%)**, and **61/870 of all answered questions (7%)**. The first coverage figure describes sampling within the five included topics; the second exposes how little of all output has been checked. Neither makes the excluded topics represented.

Keep the query and sampling-frame definition in source control under a name such as `support_coverage_v1`. Compute the numerator, denominator, exclusions and dates together. A model must not fill gaps in the daily tile with a guessed percentage. Changes to the sampled population need a visible definition version.

## Keep targeted checks separate

Northwind also checks 18 returns answers after complaints; nine agree. Pooling them into this week gives 56/79 (71%), while last week's 55/64 (86%) has no targeted checks. The apparent 15-percentage-point fall compares different selection methods.

Show **Targeted complaint checks: 9/18** separately. Use a consistent sampling frame to [compare periods](/dashboard-changes-real-or-noise). Repeated customers or review batches may require analysis that accounts for clustering even when chat IDs differ. [Statistics Canada's sampling guidance](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/edu/power-pouvoir/ch13/prob/5214899-eng.htm) explains population lists, random selection and clustered designs.

## List topics outside the sample

Northwind's never-checked topics have 71 gift-card answers, 48 wholesale answers and 33 allergen answers: **152/870 (17.5%)**. List them visibly and prioritise review by risk as well as volume; an allergen answer can matter even at lower volume.
