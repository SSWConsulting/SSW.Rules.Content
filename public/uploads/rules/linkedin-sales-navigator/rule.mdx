Reaching out to a company without knowing what's happening inside it is a guessing game. You end up talking to the wrong person, at the wrong time, about something they don't care about.

LinkedIn Sales Navigator fixes this. It shows you what a company is doing right now, who the right people to speak to are, and gives you everything you need to research a company or person in depth before you reach out.

<endIntro />

1. **Follow what a company is doing:** save target companies as accounts so Sales Navigator alerts you to new leaders, headcount growth, job ads and news. It includes a tip about headcount growth by department.
2. **Find the right people to speak to:** Lead search filters (function, seniority, recently changed jobs, recently posted), Relationship Explorer and saving several people per account as leads.
3. **Research a company or person in depth:** Account IQ, news and job ads for the company. Lead IQ, recent posts and shared experiences for the person. Good and bad example messages show the difference that research makes.

<boxEmbed
  body={<>
    **Use Sales Navigator by hand**
    
    LinkedIn doesn't allow scraping tools, bots, browser extensions that automate clicks, or bulk exporting of contacts. It can detect this activity and restrict or permanently remove your account.
    
    * Don't use tools that auto-visit profiles, auto-connect or auto-message
    * Don't copy contact lists out of Sales Navigator in bulk
    * Don't open dozens of profiles in quick succession
    
    Losing your Sales Navigator seat costs far more than the time any shortcut saves.
