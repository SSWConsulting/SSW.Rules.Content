Selling fish is cold, wet and repetitive work. At Pike Place Fish Market in Seattle, the fishmongers throw fish across the counter, joke with customers and draw a crowd every day.

The work did not change. The way the team did the work changed. The **FISH! Philosophy** turns that into 4 practices that any team can use.

<endIntro />

<youtubeEmbed url="https://www.youtube.com/embed/3azPwinY1oE" description={"Video: The world famous Pike Place Fish Market decades later. It still smells of FISH! | Tomas Bay (6 min)"} />

## The 4 practices

| Practice | What it means | At work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Be There** | Give people your full attention | Look away from your screen when someone talks to you |
| **Play** | Bring energy and fun to the work | Celebrate a win in the team chat |
| **Make Their Day** | Do a small thing that makes someone feel valued | Thank a colleague by name for a specific piece of work |
| **Choose Your Attitude** | You choose how you react, even on a bad day | Look for the reason behind a change before you complain |

## Be There

A person can tell when you only half listen. Close the chat window, look at them and let them finish.

<boxEmbed
  style="greybox"
  body={<>
    A colleague asks for help. You keep typing and say "yep, go on" without looking up.
  </>}
  figurePrefix="bad"
  figure="Bad example - You are in the room, but not there"
/>

<boxEmbed
  style="greybox"
  body={<>
    A colleague asks for help. You stop typing, turn to them and say "Sure, show me."
  </>}
  figurePrefix="good"
  figure="Good example - You give them your full attention"
/>

## Make Their Day

Small actions make the biggest difference. A specific thank you means more than a general one.

  body={<>
    thx

  figure="Bad example - A thank you that says nothing"
/>

<boxEmbed

    Thanks Sam for fixing the build at 7am. The client demo worked first time because of you 🎉

  figure="Good example - A thank you that names the person and the work"

## Choose Your Attitude

You cannot control every change. You can control how you react to it.

<boxEmbed
  style="greybox"
  body={<>
    "The client changed the scope again. This project is a joke."
  </>}
  figurePrefix="bad"
  figure="Bad example - The complaint spreads to the whole team"
/>

<boxEmbed
  style="greybox"
  body={<>
    "The client changed the scope again. Let's find out what they learnt, and update the backlog."
  </>}
  figurePrefix="good"
  figure="Good example - The team moves forward"
/>

See [Do you find the positive in the decisions?](/the-happiness-equation)

## Play

Fun at work makes people want to come back tomorrow. Keep it inclusive, so that nobody is the joke.

* Celebrate a release, a passed test or a happy client
* Mark the small moments, such as [birthdays](/celebrate-birthdays)
* Try the silly idea in a brainstorm. It can lead to the good one
