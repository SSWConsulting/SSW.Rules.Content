In today's fast-paced world, lengthy emails, web content, and instant messages can be overwhelming and difficult to navigate.

<endIntro />

## Embrace the principle of "less is more"

By being concise and focusing on relevant information only, we can capture the reader's attention and prevent important messages from being overlooked or postponed due to time constraints.

  style="greybox"
  body={<>
    "The team conducted a comprehensive evaluation of the project’s scope and objectives to determine the best course of action moving forward."
  </>}
  figurePrefix="bad"

<boxEmbed

  </>}
  figurePrefix="bad"
  figure="Cut too much - it no longer says what was reviewed or why"
/>

<boxEmbed
  style="greybox"
  body={<>
    "The team reviewed the project’s scope and goals to decide the next steps."
  </>}
  figurePrefix="good"
  figure="Short, but still says what was reviewed and why"
/>

Keep the detail your reader needs. Cut repetition and filler without removing a condition, uncertainty, or the reason for the work. A longer explanation can be appropriate when the subject needs it. If AI shortened your text, compare the revision with the original to check nothing important was lost.

> "I have made this longer than usual because I have not had time to make it shorter."
> Blaise Pascal
