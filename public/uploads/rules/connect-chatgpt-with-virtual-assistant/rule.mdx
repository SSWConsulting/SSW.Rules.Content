Apple now provides a built-in ChatGPT extension for Siri through Apple Intelligence. Use Apple's supported setup instead of relying on the older third-party video instructions. This guide explains eligibility, setup, and how to control what you share.

<endIntro />

## Check device, software, language, and region requirements

Apple introduced the ChatGPT extension with Apple Intelligence in iOS 18.2, and its current availability page lists the extension among features supported in iOS 26. You do not need iOS 27 to use the extension. It is available only on compatible Apple Intelligence devices, not every iPhone that can run those iOS versions. For iOS 27, Apple lists iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 16 models or later, and iPhone Air. Check Apple's current [Apple Intelligence device requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115) for the supported device and software combination.

Apple Intelligence requires the device language and Siri language to be set to the same supported language. Apple currently lists Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, and Vietnamese. Feature availability can vary by language and region. The ChatGPT extension is available only where the ChatGPT app and service are available.

## Set up the ChatGPT extension

1. On your iPhone, open **Settings**.
2. Follow the path for your iOS version:
   * **iOS 26:** Tap **Apple Intelligence & Siri**, then under **Extensions** tap **ChatGPT**, then **Set Up**.
   * **iOS 27:** Tap **Siri**, then **ChatGPT**, then **Set Up**.
   * **Siri AI beta on iOS 27:** Tap **Set Up Extensions**, choose **ChatGPT**, then turn on **Use Extension**. Siri AI is a separate beta experience; Apple currently lists it as English-only and initially unavailable in the EU on iPhone.
3. Follow the onscreen instructions and choose whether to sign in to a ChatGPT account. Signing in is optional. You need to sign in if you want requests saved in your ChatGPT chat history.

Apple may change settings labels and paths in later iOS releases.

## Choose when Siri can send content

Siri asks before sharing information with ChatGPT by default. In **iOS 26**, go to **Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri > ChatGPT** and turn off **Confirm ChatGPT Requests** to send ordinary requests without confirmation. Photos and files still require confirmation; starting an ordinary request with “Ask ChatGPT” can also skip confirmation. In **iOS 27**, the corresponding control is **Settings > Siri > ChatGPT > Confirm Requests**. That control is unavailable with Siri AI beta, where you must ask ChatGPT before a prompt, photo, or file is sent.

You choose what to send. When a request is sent, ChatGPT receives the request and any attachments, plus limited context such as your time zone, country, device type, language, and which feature made the request. Apple says your IP address is obscured from ChatGPT, while your general location is provided for fraud prevention and legal compliance.

Without a ChatGPT account, Apple says OpenAI does not receive information tied to your Apple Account, and must process the request only to fulfill it, without storing the request or response (except where required by law) or using it to train models. When you sign in, your ChatGPT account settings and OpenAI's privacy policies apply. Avoid sending sensitive work or personal information unless you are authorized to share it.

For instructions and details, see Apple's [iOS 26 guide to using ChatGPT with Apple Intelligence](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-chatgpt-with-apple-intelligence-iph00fd3c8c2/26/ios/26), [current iOS 27 guide to turning on ChatGPT](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-on-chatgpt-iph00fd3c8c2/ios), [Apple Intelligence availability](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115), and [iOS 18.2 announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/12/apple-intelligence-now-features-image-playground-genmoji-and-more/). This guide is based on Apple's documentation checked on 10 October 2026; settings and eligibility may change.

## Connecting ChatGPT with Gemini or Google Assistant (for Android)

Google is replacing Google Assistant with Gemini on Android, so on newer devices Gemini is your built-in conversational AI out of the box.

If you still want ChatGPT specifically as your Android voice assistant, it is a little trickier but still totally doable! [Follow these instructions](https://www.howtogeek.com/882019/how-to-use-chatgpt-like-google-assistant-on-android/) and you should be talking to your new AI assistant in no time.
