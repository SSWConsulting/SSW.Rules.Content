Apple now provides a built-in ChatGPT extension for Siri through Apple Intelligence. Use Apple's supported setup instead of relying on the older third-party video instructions. This guide explains eligibility, setup, and how to control what you share.

<endIntro />

## Check iPhone and language eligibility

Apple's current iPhone guide lists Apple Intelligence support on iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 16 models or later, and iPhone Air, with iOS 27 or later. This is not available on every iPhone that can run iOS 27. Check Apple's current [Apple Intelligence device requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115) before following the setup steps, because supported devices and software change over time.

Apple Intelligence requires the device language and Siri language to be set to the same supported language. Apple currently lists Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, and Vietnamese. Feature availability can vary by language and region. The ChatGPT extension is available only where the ChatGPT app and service are available.

## Turn on the ChatGPT extension

1. On your iPhone, open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Siri**, then **ChatGPT**.
3. Tap **Set Up** and follow the onscreen instructions.
4. Choose whether to sign in to a ChatGPT account. Signing in is optional. You need to sign in if you want requests saved in your ChatGPT chat history.

On iOS 27's Siri AI beta interface, Apple documents a different path: tap **Set Up Extensions**, choose **ChatGPT**, then turn on **Use Extension**. Interface labels can change between software releases.

## Choose when Siri can send content

You are asked before information is shared with ChatGPT by default. In the Settings app, go to **Siri > ChatGPT** and turn off **Confirm Requests** if you want Siri to send eligible requests without asking each time. Apple says this option is not available with Siri AI; you must first ask ChatGPT before a prompt, photo, or file is sent.

You choose what to send. When a request is sent, ChatGPT receives the request and any attachments, plus limited context such as your time zone, country, device type, language, and which feature made the request. Apple says your IP address is obscured from ChatGPT, while your general location is provided for fraud prevention and legal compliance.

Without a ChatGPT account, Apple says OpenAI does not receive information tied to your Apple Account, and must process the request only to fulfill it, without storing the request or response (except where required by law) or using it to train models. When you sign in, your ChatGPT account settings and OpenAI's privacy policies apply. Avoid sending sensitive work or personal information unless you are authorized to share it.

For current instructions and details, see Apple's [iPhone User Guide: Turn on ChatGPT](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-on-chatgpt-iph00fd3c8c2/ios) and [Apple Intelligence availability](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115). This guide is based on Apple's documentation checked on 10 October 2026; settings and eligibility may change.

## Connecting ChatGPT with Gemini or Google Assistant (for Android)

Google is replacing Google Assistant with Gemini on Android, so on newer devices Gemini is your built-in conversational AI out of the box.

If you still want ChatGPT specifically as your Android voice assistant, it is a little trickier but still totally doable! [Follow these instructions](https://www.howtogeek.com/882019/how-to-use-chatgpt-like-google-assistant-on-android/) and you should be talking to your new AI assistant in no time.
