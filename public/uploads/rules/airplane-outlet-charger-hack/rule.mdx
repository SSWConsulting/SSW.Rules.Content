Airplane power outlets are heavily used and can become so loose that chargers fall out easily, especially heavier laptop chargers.

<endIntro />

A simple fix is to carry a **small international travel adapter**. Plug the adapter into the airplane outlet, then plug your charger into the adapter. It often creates a much firmer connection.
