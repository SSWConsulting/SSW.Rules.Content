A badge is easy to share, but an image alone does not show who issued it or what someone did to earn it. [Credly badges](https://support.credly.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021222071-What-is-a-badge) link to details that help others verify an achievement.

<endIntro />

Use Credly when an achievement has clear criteria and the earner benefits from sharing proof of it. Decide what someone must demonstrate before issuing the badge.

## Is the subscription worth it?

**Credly subscriptions are expensive**, so be selective about which achievements need a verifiable badge. Estimate how many badges you will issue and compare that with the quoted subscription cost before committing. Credly does not publish a standard price, and [unused badges purchased for a contract year expire](https://info.credly.com/credly-frequently-asked-question).

For a one-off participation award without an assessment, a simple certificate may be enough. Use Credly when verification and sharing add value for the earner.
