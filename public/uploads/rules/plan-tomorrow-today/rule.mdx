At the end of a busy day, it's easy to sign off and not think about tomorrow until it arrives. Then at 8:30am you find out you should be at a client site across town at 9am.

Take 5 minutes before you finish each day to plan the next one.

## What to check

1. **Your calendar** - Where do you need to be, and when? If you start at a client site, plan how you'll get there
2. **Your top 3 tasks** - Pick what you'll work on, with the hardest one first. See [Do you tackle the hard things first?](/hard-tasks-first)
3. **What you need** - Prepare anything you need before a meeting, like files, access or your laptop charger

## On the move

Keep your calendar on your phone (e.g. the Outlook app), so you can check where you need to be next between meetings or on the way to a client.
