If you have to address an email to one or multiple people (including [to yourself](/dones-do-you-send-yourself-emails)), make every greeting **a heading 3**. That includes a single addressee, a group greeting such as "Hi All", and each smaller group that has its own part of the email. This helps everyone quickly find which part of the email applies to them.

  cc="Adam"
  bcc=""

    ### Hi Jean,

### Hi Anthony,

  </>}

/>

The same applies to a group email. When part of the email is for everyone and part is for a smaller group, each part gets its own heading.

Product Owners, please:

  </>}
