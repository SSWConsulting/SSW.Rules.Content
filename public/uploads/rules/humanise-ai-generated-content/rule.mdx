A rewrite can sound smoother while changing what you meant. It might turn a cautious estimate into a promise, remove an exception, or invent a personal detail to make the text feel authentic.

Use AI to improve a draft for its reader. Keep control of the facts, argument, and voice throughout the edit.

<endIntro />

## What should you give the AI?

Supply your audience, purpose, notes, sources, and any terminology that must stay precise. State what you want: an outline, a critique, possible cuts, or a rewrite.

A role can guide tone, but it does not provide expertise or lived experience. Supply your own relevant anecdotes. If a draft needs a statistic, quotation, or example you do not have, ask for a source or omit it. Never invent experience, credentials, or evidence to make writing seem more human.

For voice preferences, provide [examples of your own writing](/train-ai-to-write-in-your-style/). Share only material you are authorized to use with the tool.

## What is worth changing?

Ask for a diagnosis before a rewrite. A useful suggestion quotes the passage and explains how a change helps the reader.

Look for unsupported claims, repeated information, missing steps in the argument, and wording the audience may misunderstand. If a metaphor distracts or obscures the intended relationship, simplify it. Combining images is not automatically a mistake; judge what readers can understand in context.

Keep precise technical words and intentional rhetorical devices. A three-part list may describe exactly three requirements. A longer sentence may need its qualifications. Use [punctuation](/dashes/) and sentence rhythm to support the meaning, rather than following word blacklists or fixed patterns.

Perplexity measures predictability relative to a particular model. It is not a universal measure of human authorship or good writing. Changing sentence lengths or replacing familiar words does not prove where a draft came from.

## How do you preserve meaning?

Ask for small edits with the original and suggestion shown together. Before accepting a change, compare:

* **Claims and evidence** - Facts, numbers, dates, sources, and attribution
* **Strength and scope** - Uncertainty, exceptions, conditions, and who or what the claim covers
* **Reasoning and commitments** - Causal relationships, counterarguments, promises, and the author's position
* **Voice** - Appropriate terminology, emphasis, humor, and distinctive phrasing

A mechanical check that names and numbers match cannot establish that meaning is unchanged. Read the result and [verify its claims](/avoid-ai-hallucinations/), including after later revisions.

## When is the edit finished?

Accept changes that improve accuracy, clarity, or usefulness. Restore qualifications and voice that were lost. An effective passage may need no changes.

Keep the original so you can compare versions. Do not add fake anecdotes, deliberate mistakes, or awkward synonyms to lower a detector score. Judge the result by what it communicates, and follow the [disclosure requirements that apply](/indicate-ai-helped/).
