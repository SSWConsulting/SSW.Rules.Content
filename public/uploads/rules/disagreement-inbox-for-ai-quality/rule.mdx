Northwind Traders' example dashboard labels 14 AI–human disagreements as wrong answers and recommends a prompt change. Four answers differ only in format; three quote a retired returns policy. The headline hides different problems that need different fixes.

**Review disagreements against the evidence before changing the prompt.** Use an inbox to turn a quality rate into specific, owned repairs.

## Separate agreement from correctness

A person's answer is a comparator, not automatically ground truth. The [accuracy metric](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.metrics.accuracy_score.html) compares predictions with reference labels; its meaning depends on those labels being correct. Name the measure the evidence supports:

* **Strict agreement** — AI and human answers match under a documented comparison
* **Normalised agreement** — A secondary comparison accepts specified equivalences, such as option order. Preserve original answers and version the comparator
* **Reviewed outcomes** — A person checks the source and written rubric, then records whether AI is right, wrong or still unclear, including cases where both answers are wrong

Northwind keeps strict agreement at 47/61 (77%) and normalised agreement at 51/61. The extra four are explained comparator differences, not four newly correct AI answers. For [uncertainty and comparisons between periods](/dashboard-changes-real-or-noise), show counts, a period and an appropriate range.

Two answers can agree and both be wrong. Adjudicate a random sample of agreements too; reviewing only disagreements cannot establish overall accuracy.

## Give every disagreement evidence and an owner

Put unclassified cases first, newest first within each status. Show the question, both answers, source version, cause, owner and review status, with a link to the full context. Apply the original records' access controls and minimise personal data in the preview.

AI may suggest a cause, but a reviewer confirms it against the source. An ambiguous case stays unresolved or goes to a second reviewer; don't force it into the model-error count. Show [how much reached review](/show-coverage-on-ai-quality-dashboards) beside the rate.

## Fix the cause, then verify the repair

Northwind reviews 11 of 14 disagreements: four comparator bugs, three wrong-source cases, one ambiguous question, one human error and two model/prompt errors. Three remain new. The reviewed outcomes are five AI right, five AI wrong and one unclear; the source version explains why some human answers are wrong too.

Fix and recheck the comparator and retired article before rewriting the prompt. Close a row only after a reviewer verifies the repair. Add confirmed model errors to [the eval set](/run-evals-on-ai-features) and rerun it before release. This makes [owning AI output](/avoid-automation-bias) a repeatable review process.
