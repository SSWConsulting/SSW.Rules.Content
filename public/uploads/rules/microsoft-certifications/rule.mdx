Whether you're an expert or just getting started, working towards gaining a Microsoft certification is a worthwhile investment.

Microsoft provides numerous certifications and training options to help you:

* Learn new skills and fill technical knowledge gaps
* Prove your competence to clients, and improve your LinkedIn profile
* Boost your productivity
* Help your company with their Microsoft Partnership status

## Fundamentals

If you're just getting started, take a look at Fundamental exams, for example:

* [Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-fundamentals/)
* [Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-901)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-ai-fundamentals/)
* [Power Platform Fundamentals (PL-900)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/power-platform-fundamentals/)

***

## Associate

Most people can skip the exams above, and jump into Associate certifications.

Some popular ones are:

* [Azure AI Cloud Developer Associate (AI-200)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-ai-cloud-developer-associate/)
* [Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate (AI-103)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-ai-apps-and-agents-developer-associate/)
* [Power Platform Developer Associate (PL-400)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/power-platform-developer-associate/)
* [Azure Administrator Associate (AZ-104)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-administrator/)
* [Azure Network Engineer Associate (AZ-700)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-network-engineer-associate/)

***

## Expert

Once you have an associate certification or 2, prove your advanced skills by getting an Expert certification. Note these generally have prerequisites, for example AZ-305 requires AZ-104.

The main expert certifications are:

* [Azure Solutions Architect Expert (AZ-305)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-solutions-architect/)
* [DevOps Engineer Expert (AZ-400)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/devops-engineer/)
* [Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect (AB-100)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/agentic-ai-business-solutions-architect/)
* [Cybersecurity Architect Expert (SC-100)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/cybersecurity-architect-expert/)

***

## Specialty

Specialty exams focus on specific workloads or skills, including GitHub, Virtual Desktops, and Cosmos DB:

* [ Azure Virtual Desktop Specialty (AZ-140)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-virtual-desktop-specialty/)
* [GitHub Administration (GH-100)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/github-administration/)
* [Agentic AI Developer (GH-600)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/agentic-ai-developer/)
* [Azure Cosmos DB Developer Specialty (DP-420)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-cosmos-db-developer-specialty/)

## Tips for preparing

Preparing for exams can involve a lot of work, but remember, you're not in school anymore! You've chosen to take this exam, and no one is forcing you. So just sit back and **enjoy the journey - you should feel excited by the new skills you will soon learn**.

Here are some quick tips to help you pass:

1. Take practice tests, e.g. from [Mindhub](https://au.mindhub.com/microsoft-exam-replay-with-practice-test-mcp-exam/p/Microsoft-Exam-Replay-PT)
2. Pair up - studying with someone else is good as you can hold each other accountable, and it’s just more enjoyable!
3. [Attend SSW training](https://www.ssw.com.au/events) - have a look at when the next SSW Azure Super Skills is being held in your city and book it in! These are great and offer a wealth of knowledge about the certification process and can make your time getting certified easier.

Good luck!

| Azure AI Engineer Associate (AI-102)                  | June 30, 2026       | [Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate (AI-103)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-ai-apps-and-agents-developer-associate/)                    |
