Introducing a critical agent can help refine the output generated by ChatGPT. By asking the model to critique its own responses and revise them based on the feedback, you can improve the overall quality and usefulness of the information or assistance you receive.

<endIntro />

## Polish routine drafts with self-critique

For clarity and polish in routine drafts, ask the same model to critique and revise its response. These tips use ChatGPT, but also work with other chat models:

* Make it clear that you want ChatGPT to critique its own output
* Specify the aspects you want the model to focus on when evaluating its response
* Encourage the model to make revisions based on the critique

Self-critique can improve clarity and usefulness, but it can also miss errors in the original response.

## Give consequential content an independent critique

When people will publish, reuse or rely on the content, go beyond self-critique and seek an independent challenge:

1. **Start a fresh review.** Provide the draft, brief, acceptance criteria and sources without the drafting conversation. Use a different model family where available; otherwise, start a fresh chat with the same model. The same model may retain its blind spots; agreement between models is not proof.
2. **Ask for evidence.** Request unsupported claims, missing facts and misleading examples. For each finding, ask for the affected passage, a source reference where available and a specific correction. Ask the reviewer to flag missing evidence rather than invent references.
3. **Verify before revising.** Have a person open the cited sources, check that they support the claims and resolve disagreements against the evidence. Apply focused corrections without removing the reasons behind the advice.
4. **Run deterministic checks.** Check word limits, required items, formatting and links with tools. These checks can confirm that a required statement appears; a person still needs to check its accuracy against the source.

A short routine message rarely needs this full process. Match the review effort to the content's reach and consequences.
