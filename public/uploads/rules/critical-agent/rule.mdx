Introducing a critical agent can help refine the output generated by ChatGPT. By asking the model to critique its own responses and revise them based on the feedback, you can improve the overall quality and usefulness of the information or assistance you receive.

<endIntro />

Here are some tips for introducing a critical agent:

* Make it clear that you want ChatGPT to critique its own output
* Specify the aspects you want the model to focus on when evaluating its response
* Encourage the model to make revisions based on the critique

By introducing a critical agent, you can ensure that the model not only generates content but also reviews and refines it, leading to more polished and high-quality responses.

## Give consequential content an independent critique

For published or reused content, review the draft in a fresh context with the brief, acceptance criteria and source material. Use a reviewer from a different model family where available to reduce dependence on the writer's own preferences. This supplies another perspective, not proof that either model is correct.

Ask the reviewer to identify unsupported claims, missing facts, misleading examples and specific revisions, with source references. Have a person follow those references and resolve disagreements against the evidence before accepting changes. If only one model is available, a fresh chat still removes the drafting conversation, although the same model's preferences remain.

Use deterministic checks for word limits, required items, formatting and links. A script can establish that a required fact appears; source checking establishes whether it is true. Keep edits focused so polishing does not remove the reasons behind the advice. A short routine message rarely needs this full process; use it where the content's reach or consequences justify the review.
