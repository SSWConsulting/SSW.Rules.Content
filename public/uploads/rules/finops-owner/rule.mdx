Most teams set up [budgets and cost alerts](/azure-budgets) in Azure, but often nobody is responsible for acting on them. When an alert arrives, everyone assumes someone else is looking into it, and the cost only gets attention when the Product Owner asks about the bill.

That's why every project should have a **FinOps owner** - one named person who is responsible for the project's cloud costs.

## What the FinOps owner does

1. **Acts on the alerts** - Makes sure budget and [cost anomaly](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/understand/analyze-unexpected-charges) alerts [go to the project's Teams channel](/set-up-azure-alert-emails-on-teams-channel), and is the person who acts on them
2. **Reviews the bill every month** - Compares actual spend in [Cost analysis](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/costs/quick-acm-cost-analysis) with the budget and the original estimate, and can explain the difference
3. **Acts on Azure Advisor** - Reads the [cost recommendations](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/advisor/advisor-reference-cost-recommendations) and adds the ones worth doing to the backlog
4. **Keeps the Product Owner informed** - Reports the month's spend, and [warns them before an expected spike](/spike-in-azure-resource-costs)
5. **Hands the role over** - Names the next owner before leaving the project

## Who should it be?

Pick one person, not a team. The Tech Lead is usually the right choice: they know what every resource is for, and [monitoring the budget](/know-the-non-scrum-roles) is already part of the role. The Product Owner still approves the spend.

They don't need to be an Owner of the subscription. Cost Management Reader is enough to see costs and budgets, and Cost Management Contributor lets them create budgets and anomaly alerts.

The [Azure Spend Master](/do-you-have-an-azure-spend-master) controls who can create resources. The FinOps owner watches what one project's resources cost each month.

## Write it down

Put the FinOps owner's name in the `Owner` tag of the project's resource group. Anyone who opens the resource group in Azure then knows who to ask about the cost. See [Do you apply Tags to your Azure Resource Groups?](/apply-tags-to-your-azure-resource-groups)
