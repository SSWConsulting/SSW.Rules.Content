A lot of your audience will not always be looking at your video. A sweet sound can make users look at the video.

<endIntro />

### Use a paste sound to highlight content additions

When you paste text, code snippets, or configurations during a fast-paced demo, viewers can easily miss where the new content came from. A quick, subtle paste sound draws immediate attention to the change on the screen.

<youtubeEmbed description="✅ Video: Good example - Audio cue highlights the exact moment content is pasted [1:55 - 1:56]. " url="https://youtu.be/3t4v91RrDH0" />

### Use a typing sound to make on-screen typing feel alive

When you show someone typing a command, a prompt, or a line of code, silent keystrokes make the screen feel flat and pre-recorded. A light typing sound tells viewers that something is being entered right now, and keeps their eyes on the cursor instead of drifting.

<youtubeEmbed url="https://youtu.be/0UJoG9SEVKw" />

✅ Video: Good example — Typing sound follows the keystrokes as the prompt is entered \[1:32 - 1:35].

### Use audio cues to highlight important milestone events

Audio cues are not only useful when text appears — they are also powerful when highlighting **important milestone events** during a demo or tutorial.

Viewers are often multitasking or looking away from the screen. A short sound effect acts as an **attention trigger**, letting them know something meaningful has just happened. This is especially valuable when the event involves **executables or results**, rather than simple navigation.

Examples of milestone events include:

* Clicking Deploy to release an application
* Clicking Submit to send data or complete a form
* Clicking Generate to create a report, document, or artifact
* Clicking Build to compile a project
* Clicking Create when a new resource (project, repository, environment) is being created
* Clicking Connect or Authenticate to establish a service connection
* Clicking Start recording or Start demo in a tutorial
* Clicking Install to start installing software

These moments represent **milestones in the story of the demo**. Adding a subtle audio cue reinforces that something important occurred.
