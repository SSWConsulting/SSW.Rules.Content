Often times it can be tempting to say ‘yes’ just simply to be polite or to keep the conversation moving, but this can cause a serious break in communication.

Imagine this scenario, a client explains a new feature to you but some of the requirements are unclear, the developer says “Yep, sounds good to me” to be polite and avoid slowing down the conversation. The developer then builds what the client wanted, but during the sprint review the client says “That's not what I meant." 

… suddenly you have an unhappy client, because you've now cost them extra for your mistake and wasted valuable development time.

<youtubeEmbed url="https://www.youtube.com/shorts/4KDQghJL5g8" description="Figure: Communication tip from Adam (2 min)" />

As a consultant or developer, you should always be asking follow up questions. It is not rude to say no or follow-up when you don't understand the question or requirement.
