A pull request that says "I picked this LLM" shows a decision, not an experiment. An R&D assessor needs to see what you tried, what you measured, and why you chose what you chose. Without measured results, you cannot prove the work was experimental.

<endIntro />

## Make the outcome measurable before you start

Decide how you will judge success **before** running the experiment. This is your benchmark, and it comes from your core activity. See [how to define your core activities for R&D](/do-you-define-your-core-activities).

For each experiment, define:

* **The input** - the same data for every option (for example, one call transcript)
* **The expected result** - what a correct answer looks like (for example, the list of tasks you know are in the call)
* **The measure** - how you will score each option (for example, the percentage of expected tasks found)

## Compare options, don't just pick one

Test several options against the same input and expected result, then record the results side by side.

## Document your results

You need to be able to **prove what happened**. For every experiment:

* Record the scores or outcomes, including for the options you did not choose
* Say what you learned, even when the experiment failed. See [documenting your failures for R&D](/do-you-record-your-failures)
* Keep the results with the PBI, not only in a chat or on your machine. See [recording your research under the PBI](/do-you-record-your-research-under-the-pbi)

## Lead the report with the result and its boundaries

Summarize the measured comparison above in four short statements so readers see the result and its boundaries together:

* **Verdict** - State the finding and the measure that supports it. Use **Inconclusive** when the evidence cannot distinguish the options
* **Scope** - Identify the compared versions or configurations, data, sample size and environment. Distinguish repeated runs from independent examples
* **Limits** - State exclusions and uncertainty, including when no valid uncertainty estimate is available
* **Decision** - Name the next action, owner and review date or condition. Mark unapproved recommendations as pending

Follow the opening with the hypothesis, success criteria, method, full results and reproducible evidence. Before sharing, read only the opening and check it still says what was tested, what remains uncertain and who acts next.

Failed experiments still count as R&D. A clearly recorded "this did not work, and here is the data" is valuable evidence.
