Until November 2026 this rule recommended Sora and DALL-E 3. Both had already been shut down. A hand-written list of "the best AI tools" goes stale fast, and every vendor's launch post claims their tool is number one.

The fix is to check independent leaderboards, then test the top few on your own work. This rule shows you which leaderboards to trust, where they mislead, and the tools that are winning right now.

<endIntro />

## Why can't you trust the hype, or a single score?

A benchmark is a fixed set of tasks that every AI model is scored on. A leaderboard ranks the models by those scores. Both are useful, and both can fool you:

* **Vendors pick their best numbers** - A launch post shows the 6 benchmarks the model won, not the 20 it lost
* **Models memorise the test** - In February 2026 OpenAI stopped reporting SWE-bench Verified, a popular coding benchmark, after finding models could repeat the fixes from memory
* **The test itself can be broken** - In the same audit, 59.4% of the hard tasks OpenAI checked had tests that rejected correct answers
* **Leaderboards get gamed** - The Leaderboard Illusion paper found Meta privately tested 27 model variants on Arena, then published only the best one
* **Small gaps are noise** - Anthropic measured a 6 point swing on a coding benchmark from changing nothing but the hardware limits. On a coding benchmark, treat a gap under 3 percentage points as a tie. On Arena, check whether the ± ranges overlap
* **Leaderboards disagree** - In November 2026, Arena and Artificial Analysis each named a different winner for text-to-video

## Which leaderboards should you check?

| Leaderboard | What it tells you | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Arena](https://arena.ai/leaderboard) (Recommended) | Which answer people prefer in blind votes, for text, web dev, images, video and search | People vote for what they like, not what is correct. New models show as "Preliminary" with few votes |
| [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/) | Intelligence vs speed vs price from tests it runs itself, plus image, video, speech and music | One combined score hides which tasks a model is bad at |
| [Terminal-Bench](https://www.tbench.ai/leaderboard) | Which coding agent and model pair solves the most real terminal tasks, with cost | Scores move with the hardware used |
| [OpenRouter rankings](https://openrouter.ai/rankings) | What people actually use, by tokens processed through OpenRouter | Popular is not the same as best. Cheap and free models rank high |
| [Vals AI](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks) | Independent scores on legal, finance and healthcare tasks | Only helps if the task matches yours |

## How do you pick a tool using benchmarks?

1. **Match the leaderboard to the task** - Writing an email is Arena's text board. Making a video is the text-to-video board. A general "smartest model" score tells you little about either
2. **Check a second leaderboard** - If two independent leaderboards agree, trust it more. If they disagree, test both winners
3. **Shortlist 2 or 3** - Ignore small gaps at the top
4. **Run your own mini benchmark** - Put 5 to 10 real tasks from your week in a spreadsheet. Run each tool on all of them and note which output you would send to a client
5. **Check price and privacy** - A tool that wins by 1% and costs 5 times more is rarely the right pick
6. **Re-check every 3 months** - Put a reminder in your calendar. The winner changes

### Writing and research

* ⭐💲 **[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com)** - The all-rounder for drafting emails, brainstorming, and answering questions
* ⭐💲 **[Claude](https://claude.ai)** - Strong at long documents, careful writing, and code
* 💲 **[Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/)** - Best if you live in Gmail, Docs, and Google Cloud
* 💲 **[Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai)** - Search answers with citations you can check
* 💲 **[Notion AI](https://www.notion.com/product/ai)** - Writing help, summaries, and search inside your Notion workspace

### Coding

* ⭐💲 **[Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code)** - A coding agent that runs in your terminal. Top of Terminal-Bench in November 2026
* ⭐💲 **[OpenAI Codex](https://openai.com/codex/)** - OpenAI's coding agent, close behind on Terminal-Bench
* 💲 **[Cursor](https://cursor.com/)** - An AI code editor that understands your whole codebase
* 💲 **[GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot)** - AI help inside VS Code, Visual Studio, and GitHub, with a free tier
* **[OpenCode](https://opencode.ai/)** - An open source coding agent that works with models from 200+ providers, so you are not locked to one vendor

### Presentation design

* ⭐💲 **[Gamma](https://gamma.app)** - Turns an outline into a designed deck in minutes
* ⭐💲 **[Napkin](https://www.napkin.ai/)** - Turns your text into diagrams and visuals
* 💲 **[Canva](https://www.canva.com/)** - AI slide and design generation inside a tool your marketing team already uses
* 💲 **[Beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai)** - Lays out your slides for you as you add content
* 💲 **[Pitch](https://pitch.com)** - A collaborative presentation workspace with AI slide design

### Image and design creation

* ⭐💲 **[ChatGPT Images](https://chatgpt.com)** - Top of both the Arena and Artificial Analysis text-to-image leaderboards in November 2026. Replaces DALL-E 3
* ⭐💲 **[Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com)** - Still the pick for artistic, stylised images
* **[Google Flow](https://flow.google.com/)** - Google's creative studio for images and video. Replaces ImageFX
* 💲 **[Adobe Firefly](https://firefly.adobe.com/)** - Generative AI built into Photoshop and the Adobe apps
* **[Leonardo AI](https://leonardo.ai)** - Art and design assets for games and creative projects
* **[Stable Diffusion](https://stability.ai/stable-image)** - An open model you can run on your own hardware

### Video editing and creation

* ⭐ **[Google Flow](https://flow.google.com/)** - Text-to-video with Google's Veo models. Replaces VideoFX
* ⭐💲 **[Runway](https://runway.com)** - AI video generation and editing, like object removal and background replacement
* 💲 **[Descript](https://www.descript.com/)** - Edit a video by editing its transcript
* 💲 **[Lumen5](https://lumen5.com)** - Turns a blog post into a video summary
* 💲 **[Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io)** - Videos with AI avatars and voiceovers

### Audio and music generation

* ⭐💲 **[ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/)** - Realistic text-to-speech and voice cloning
* ⭐💲 **[Suno](https://suno.com/)** - Full songs with vocals from a text prompt. Top of the Artificial Analysis music leaderboard in November 2026
* **[Google Flow Music](https://www.flowmusic.app/)** - Make music in seconds, free to start. Replaces MusicFX

### Productivity and automation

* ⭐💲 **[YakShaver](https://yakshaver.ai)** - Turns a screen recording into a task and sends it to the right team
* ⭐💲 **[Otter.ai](https://otter.ai)** - Meeting transcription and summaries for Teams, Zoom, and Google Meet
* 💲 **[Zapier](https://zapier.com)** - Automates workflows between thousands of apps
* 💲 **[n8n](https://n8n.io/)** - Workflow automation that is free if you host it yourself
