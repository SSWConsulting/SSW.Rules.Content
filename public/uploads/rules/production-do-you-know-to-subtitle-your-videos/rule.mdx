Captions make videos accessible to people who are deaf or hard of hearing, viewers watching without sound, and people who find the spoken language difficult to follow. They also make unclear audio easier to understand.

YouTube can generate captions automatically, but speech recognition can get names, technical terms, acronyms, punctuation, and timing wrong. Treat auto-generated captions as a starting point, not the finished result. Always review and correct them.

## How to correct YouTube's auto-generated captions

Once YouTube has finished generating the automatic caption track:

1. Sign in to [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/)
2. In the content section, select your video

<imageEmbed alt="Youtube studio showing content chooser" size="large" showBorder={false} figurePrefix="none" figure="Selecting video in Content Section" src="/uploads/rules/production-do-you-know-to-subtitle-your-videos/SCR-20261001-mmnx.png" />

3\. Find the video's language and, in the **Subtitles** column, select **Edit**

<imageEmbed alt="Image" size="large" showBorder={false} figurePrefix="none" figure="Selecting video language" src="/uploads/rules/production-do-you-know-to-subtitle-your-videos/SCR-20261001-mnuu.png" />

4\. For automatic captions, select **Duplicate and edit**, then **Continue**

<imageEmbed alt="Duplicate and edit subtitles button" size="large" showBorder={false} figurePrefix="none" figure="Duplicate and edit image" src="/uploads/rules/production-do-you-know-to-subtitle-your-videos/SCR-20261001-mpac.png" />

5\. Play the entire video and correct the caption text

6\. Check the timing and adjust the timestamps when captions appear too early or late

<imageEmbed alt="Editing the subtitles" size="large" showBorder={false} figurePrefix="none" figure="Editing the subtitles" src="/uploads/rules/production-do-you-know-to-subtitle-your-videos/SCR-20261001-mlbs.png" />

7\. Select **Publish**

<imageEmbed alt="Hitting publish" size="large" showBorder={false} figurePrefix="none" figure="Publish button" src="/uploads/rules/production-do-you-know-to-subtitle-your-videos/SCR-20261001-mvaq.png" />

8\. Watch the published video with the sound off and captions on to complete a final check

<imageEmbed alt="Final video" size="large" showBorder={false} figurePrefix="none" figure="Watching the final video" src="/uploads/rules/production-do-you-know-to-subtitle-your-videos/SCR-20261001-mvtj.png" />

Pay particular attention to:

* People's names, company names, and product names
* Technical terms, acronyms, URLs, and code
* Punctuation and capitalization
* Missing or incorrect words
* Caption timing and readable line breaks
* Speaker changes and important non-speech audio
