Modal dialogs often feel like separate parts of your application. Including your product's logo in the dialog header provides a subtle visual cue that keeps users oriented and reinforces your brand. It's a small detail, but one that makes your application feel more polished and consistent.

<endIntro />

## Benefits

* Reinforces your brand throughout the application
* Makes dialogs instantly recognizable
* Helps users stay oriented when multiple windows are open
* Creates a more polished, professional experience
* Gives the UI a consistent visual identity

❌ Bad - The dialog could belong to any application.

✅ Good - The logo immediately identifies the dialog as part of your application.
