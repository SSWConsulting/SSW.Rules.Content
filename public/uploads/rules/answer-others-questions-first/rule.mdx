It's easy to jump into a group chat, type your question and hit send. But if someone asked a question last week and nobody answered it, your new message pushes it even further up the chat. The question gets buried, the person who asked is still blocked, and it looks like nobody is reading the chat.

<endIntro />

A chat only works when questions get answers. Taking a few seconds to look after the questions already in the chat unblocks your teammates sooner, shows you are reading what others write, and stops important questions from quietly getting lost.

## Scroll up before you ask

Before you post a new question in a Teams chat, scroll up and check for any questions that never got a reply.

  src="/uploads/rules/answer-others-questions-first/bad-new-question-buries-old-one.png"

## Follow up the old question first

If you find an unanswered question, follow it up before you ask your own, even when the question was for someone else. Reply to the original message so the context is quoted, and @mention the person who needs to answer.

Once the old question is answered, go ahead and ask yours.

## Ask what happened to discussions that went quiet

Sometimes a discussion starts, a few people reply, and then it just stops. There is no decision and no clear next step. If you are not sure what became of it, ask for the status before you move on.

<imageEmbed
  src="/uploads/rules/answer-others-questions-first/good-ask-status-of-quiet-discussion.png"
