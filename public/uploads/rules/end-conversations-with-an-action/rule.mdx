A friendly call that ends with "Great chat, talk soon" often goes nowhere. Nobody owns the next step, and a week later the client is still waiting.

End every conversation by agreeing on an action: who does what, and by when. Then confirm it in writing.

<endIntro />

## Agree on the next step before you hang up

Summarize the action and check the other person agrees. For example: "So I'll send you the updated quote by Friday, and you'll check it with your manager."

Then send an ["As per our conversation" email](/as-per-our-conversation-emails) so you both have a record. If the plan changes after you send it, [send a v2](/email-send-a-v2).

## What to action after a call

Here are some more examples of typical things you action after a call:

* Send an Outlook appointment for any meeting you agreed
* Add or update the contact in your CRM
* Hand the enquiry to the right person, with your notes
* Create a task or PBI and start work
* Book a follow-up call or reminder in your calendar

On Teams calls, [AI meeting notes](/use-ai-for-meeting-notes) can capture the agreed actions for you.
