A finished tender response can run to 80 pages or more. The Bid Owner has to approve the submission at Gate 2, often with only a few hours to spare. They will not read every page, so they approve it without seeing what the team has committed to.

<endIntro />

Before Gate 2, the Bid Manager records a short Tender Summary video that walks through the final response.

## When to record it

Record it before the [Gate 2 - Submit](/tender-gates) meeting, at least 2 days before close (see [the tender timeline](/tender-timeline)). This leaves time to act on feedback.

## What to cover

Keep it to about 5 minutes:

1. **What you are offering** - the solution and the team
2. **The price** - the total, the pricing model, and what is included and excluded
3. **The Win Themes** - and where the response shows them
4. **Commitments and risks** - anything the business must deliver if you win
5. **What needs approval** - any decision the Bid Owner must make at Gate 2

## Where to put it

Save it in the tender's document folder and email the link to the Bid Owner with the Gate 2 meeting invite. Keep it after the tender closes. If you win, it is a quick briefing for the delivery team at Gate 3.

**Tip:** The [Tender Plan video](/tender-plan-video) shows the plan at the start. The Tender Summary video shows the final response at the end. Record both for every tender.
