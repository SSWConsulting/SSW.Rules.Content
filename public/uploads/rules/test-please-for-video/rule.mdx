Before a video goes live, send a "Test Please" to your managers, the people in the video, and a test group. They can catch mistakes and inconsistencies while they're still easy to fix, and check that the video meets your standards before it gets a ["Test Passed"](/video-editing-terms#25-test-passed-aka-picture-locked).

<endIntro />

1. **Complete the video editing (v1).** Follow the post-production rules, e.g. [Do you use cutaways?](/use-cutaways) and [Do you add a Call to Action?](/production-do-you-add-a-call-to-action)
2. **Upload your first edit to YouTube as Unlisted**
3. **Send a "Test Please"** to your **managers**, any **people on the video** and **one of the test groups** (designers and technical experts about the video subject), as per [Do you know how to request a "Test Please"?](/request-a-test-please)
   * **Internal testers** - Use a group chat in Teams, with each piece of feedback in its own message (see the [Teams chat example](#sample-feedback-teams-chat-internal) below)
   * **External testers** (e.g. the client) - Use email (see the [email example](#sample-feedback-email-external) below)
   * Feedback should include timestamps and use the right [video editing terms](/video-editing-terms)
   * Send it to 6 people, and you need 3 responses to move ahead
   * Testers have 48 hours. If you don't get 3 responses in that time, move to the next step
4. **Make the changes** from the feedback and re-upload to YouTube
5. **Reply to each piece of feedback** with "Done", or a reason why it's "Not Done", as per [Do you reply 'Done' and delete the original email?](/reply-done)
6. **Go live** once your manager replies ["Test Passed"](/video-editing-terms#25-test-passed-aka-picture-locked)

## Sample feedback Teams chat (internal)

When giving feedback to your own team, post it in Teams and put each point in its own message. That way, everyone can react with 👍 to the points they agree with, and the editor can see which feedback matters most.
