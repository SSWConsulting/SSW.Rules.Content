Sometimes people fire a few questions at you and the tendency is to answer in all kinds of ways that can make our signal to noise ratio poor.

Tips when answering IMs:

* Answer each question in order

* Repeat the keywords of each question

* Avoid grouping your messages together [as per the rule](/separate-messages)

## Replying to numbered tasks

Reply to numbered tasks on IM with one message per task, in the sender's order. Quote the task with its number, then put its status underneath, as per [Do you reply "Done"?](/reply-done): "✅ Done" with a link, "Partially done" with what's left and when, or "Not done" with the reason. Keep the original numbers so the sender can match each answer to its task.

If you send one link for task 1, the sender can't tell whether you saw task 2.
