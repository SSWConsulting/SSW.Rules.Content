AI can contribute to a piece of work in several ways, from checking spelling to drafting the whole text. When describing that contribution, describe what the AI did and follow any disclosure requirement that applies.

Use a shorthand only when your readers understand it.

<endIntro />

## Benefits of attributing AI

* **Adoption** - Spread and encourage usage of AI by showing how and when you use it, enabling others to think about when they can use it too
* **Transparency** - Others can identify AI-generated content, promoting openness and trust
* **Responsibility** - Encourages responsible use of AI-generated content
* **Accountability** - Describe the assistance while retaining responsibility for checking the work. Attribution alone does not establish the cause of an error (see [avoiding automation bias](/avoid-automation-bias) and [using AI responsibly](/use-ai-responsibly))

## What determines whether you disclose AI use?

Follow the policy, agreement, or publication requirements that apply. Describe the assistance accurately when disclosure is required. Checking or rewording output does not remove a disclosure requirement. You remain responsible for the final work whether or not attribution is required.

* **External communication** - Follow the disclosure requirements agreed with the client or publisher. Use a plain-language description when the reader needs to understand how AI contributed
* **When AI use is routine** - A separate label may add little when readers already know the workflow and no requirement calls for one. You remain responsible for quality. Provide disclosure when it is required or when the contribution is relevant to the reader's decision

## The 🤖 emoji

For readers who understand this shorthand, you can use the 🤖 emoji to indicate that AI helped. An emoji is not a substitute for a required description of the assistance.

You should draw a distinction between “AI helped” and “AI wholly wrote this”.

Where your team has agreed to this one/two-emoji convention, two robot emojis indicate that AI generated the entire text. Use plain language when the reader needs a clear description.
