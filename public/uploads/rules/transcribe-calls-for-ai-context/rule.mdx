On the first call with a new client, they explain how their systems fit together, who owns what, and what has gone wrong before. It is far more than anyone can write down. A week later half of it is gone, and the AI agent helping with the project never heard any of it.

Run an AI note-taker on every call, keep the full transcript, and point your AI agent at it. Everything the client has said becomes context your agent can use.

<endIntro />

## Keep the transcript, not just the summary

A summary is someone else's guess at what mattered. The detail you need 3 weeks later, like a field name, a deadline or a throwaway "we tried that in 2022", is usually the detail the summary dropped.

## Ask for consent every time

Tell everyone on the call that you are transcribing it and why, and wait for a yes. See [Do you ask for consent to record a Teams meeting?](/consent-to-record)

* **Bot-less note-takers give no warning** - Tools that listen to your laptop's audio don't join the call, so nobody sees a "recording started" banner. Telling people is your job
* **Already recorded** - If someone has started the Teams recording or transcription, everyone already knows the call is being captured. Mention your note-taker anyway
* **In person** - Ask before you start a recorder or open your laptop
* **If anyone says no** - Turn it off and [take notes by hand](/take-effective-notes)

## Run your own note-taker even when the meeting is recorded

A Teams recording does not mean you can get the transcript:

* **Only organizers can download it** - By default, [only the meeting organizer and co-organizers can download the transcript](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/view-live-transcription-in-microsoft-teams-meetings-dc1a8f23-2e20-4684-885e-2152e06a4a8b). On a call the client organized, that is the client
* **It comes as `.docx` or `.vtt`** - Both need converting before an agent can read them easily
* **You have to wait** - You can't hand it to your agent while the call is still running

## Choose a note-taker

The approach matters more than the tool. Pick one that hands you the full transcript as text, on the free tier, without sending a bot into the client's meeting.

### Recommended - Yogurt

[Yogurt](https://yogurt.so) has the best free tier for this job:

* **No limits** - No minute caps, no history window, no paid tier to be pushed towards
* **The full transcript, 3 ways** - Copy it, save it as Markdown, or let your agent read it through the local MCP server
* **Nothing leaves your laptop** - Transcription and summaries run on local models, so client conversations are never sent to a vendor or used for training
* **No bot** - Nothing joins the client's meeting

It has trade-offs. It is a young open-source project with no vendor behind it and no security certification. Its MCP server is off by default, and while it is on, any app running under your account can read your transcripts. Turn it on only when you need it.

If your laptop can't run it, use Granola and opt out of model training. For in-person conversations, use a Plaud recorder or the note-taker on your laptop.

## Keep transcripts on your machine

Calls contain things that should not spread: pricing, people's names, opinions about a vendor, sometimes a password read out loud. Keep transcripts in a local folder per client, outside any repository:

```text
~/notes/
  northwind/
    2026-11-19-spec-review-kickoff.md
    2026-11-21-integration-walkthrough.md
  contoso/
    2026-11-20-sprint-review.md
```

* **One file per call** - Name it with the date and the topic
* **Don't commit raw transcripts** - Anything in a repository is shared with everyone who can clone it. If a transcript belongs with the code, like a [Sprint Review](/keep-the-why-from-ai-assisted-development), review it and remove anything confidential first
* **Follow the client's rules** - If the client restricts where their data can go, that covers transcripts too. See [Do you store sensitive information securely?](/store-sensitive-information-securely)

## Point your AI agent at the folder

Transcripts only help if your agent reads them. Tell it where they are in your [personal instructions file](/claude-local-md), not the shared [AGENTS.md](/write-agents-md), because the folder exists only on your machine:

```markdown
## Client context
- Call transcripts for this project: ~/notes/northwind/
- Search them before asking me what the client said or wants
```
