When you review a content change, you need to see the page as it will look on the site. A raw MDX diff only shows which lines changed, and GitHub's markdown preview cannot render MDX components. Review the change in your CMS visual editor, where you see the real page and can fix it in the same place.

<endIntro />

<boxEmbed
  style="greybox"
  body={<>

    ```diff
    --- a/content/posts/welcome-alex.mdx
    +++ b/content/posts/welcome-alex.mdx
    @@ -8,3 +8,8 @@
     ---

    -Welcome **Alex** to the team. They start on Monday.
    +Welcome **Alex** to the Design team. They start on Monday.
    +
    +<Callout type="info">
    +  Alex will sit with the Design team and work on the public website.
    +</Callout>
    ```
  </>}
