When someone asks a yes or no question, a long answer makes them dig for what they want to know, and it can sound like you are avoiding the question. Start with 'Yes' or 'No', then follow with the details.

Saying 'No' can feel uncomfortable, especially when you should have already done it. Don't get defensive. [Acknowledge it, apologize and explain what you will do next](/how-to-take-feedback-or-criticism).
