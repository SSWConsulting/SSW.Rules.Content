LinkedIn is the main business and employment-oriented social networking service. It's important to keep it up-to-date and to link back to your company's services.

<endIntro />

The featured section on your profile is the best way to highlight your skills and link back to the services you or your company can provide. The easiest way to get started is to follow the [LinkedIn guide on adding sections to your profile](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a540837/add-sections-to-your-profile).
