When you publish a video to YouTube, their voice recognition system automatically generates Closed Captions (CC) for you. This is great, but it can be inaccurate or mishear you on occasion.

This is why you should use the YouTube Studio video editor, to edit your subtitles manually. This makes your video much more accessible, and a lot cleaner for those reading along.
