Booking the first available developers for a three-month project can mean missing someone with specialist skills the client needs. Another Account Manager may know about that experience, a previous client relationship, or a better team combination.

A quick discussion with the other Account Managers and a senior decision-maker helps you consider those options before committing the team.

<endIntro />

## How is this similar to Bench Masters?

[Bench Masters](/bench-master) coordinate developers on internal projects, balancing business priorities, skills and developer interests. Client Masters applies the same idea to substantial client bookings through a quick discussion among Account Managers and the relevant senior decision-maker. The Account Manager responsible for the client owns the decision.

## When should you discuss the booking?

Before booking developers for a new project lasting more than 1 month or involving 3+ developers, have a quick group chat with the other Account Managers and the relevant senior decision-maker. At SSW, include Adam in this discussion so he can contribute knowledge of specialist skills and client history.

Check with the proposed developers first, following the [3 T's: Time, Tech and Team](/book-developers-for-a-project).

Share:

* **The work** - The client, project and expected duration
* **Your recommendation** - The proposed developers and why they fit
* **Alternatives** - Other developers considered and any trade-offs
* **The deadline** - When you need to make the decision

Use Teams or your usual group chat. If a quick call would settle it faster, call.

Small bookings, routine extensions and minor team changes don't need this discussion. Revisit it if an extension or change substantially increases the commitment.

## How do you keep the decision moving?

These decisions usually need to happen within the hour. Give a specific deadline for input. The Account Manager owns the decision and proceeds if others aren't available, then posts the outcome in the chat. No vote or separate approval is required.
