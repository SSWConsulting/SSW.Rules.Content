Ideally, every phone call and meeting should be followed up with a written confirmation, creating a clear record of the decisions made and action items agreed upon.

Just as importantly, **get your ducks in a row before you hit the ☎️ button**. Preparing for a call or meeting helps keep the conversation focused, efficient, and productive. As the saying goes, **fail to plan, plan to fail**.

<endIntro />

In reality, though, we often finish one meeting or call and immediately move on to the next task, leaving the follow-up until later or forgetting it altogether.

To make preparation and follow-up part of the process, use the following workflow:

1. Prior to speaking to a client, speak to relevant people to help you formulate your recommendations e.g. speak to a developer about the proposal
2. Draft an email with bullet points for each issue (don't send)
3. Call up the client to get a [checked-by](/checked-by-xxx/)
4. Send the email to the client (Cc relevant people e.g. the developer) saying "[As per our conversation...](/do-you-send-as-per-our-conversation-emails)"

You'll refine and beautify it after the conversation.
