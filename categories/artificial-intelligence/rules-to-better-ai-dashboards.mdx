A polished dashboard can still show sample data, mistake a failed feed for zero, or recommend a fix the evidence does not support. These rules cover dashboards built with AI and dashboards used to monitor AI features.

Start by checking where the figures come from and whether they are complete. Then show the uncertainty behind changes and forecasts. For AI features, measure cost per useful outcome and make it clear which answers were checked and which disagreements need review.

Much of this is ordinary dashboard discipline, so also see [Rules to Better Business Intelligence](/rules-to-better-business-intelligence).
