AI can build a polished dashboard in an afternoon. Making people trust its numbers takes longer: every figure needs a source, every change needs evidence, and AI-written insights must quote the data, not invent it.

These rules cover building dashboards with AI, keeping their numbers honest, and using dashboards to track your AI features' quality and cost.
