AI can build a polished dashboard in an afternoon. To make it worth trusting, read every figure from its source, check the totals, make missing or stale data visible, and say when there is too little evidence to judge a change.

These rules cover building dashboards with AI and using dashboards to watch your AI features: grounding AI-written insights in recorded data, testing forecasts against past results, measuring cost per useful outcome, and showing how much of the AI's work was actually checked.
